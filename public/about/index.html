Features

Built for listening, not for configuring

Folders with subfolders

Import a whole folder: the tree stays on the left and your playlist on the right.

Reorderable playlists

Multiple playlists, drag to reorder, and double-click a folder to add all of it.

Cover art editor

Move, zoom, stretch, rotate and skew the image, then crop to 1:1 for a perfect fit.

Tags and export

Edit title, artist, album and genre. For MP3, export a copy with ID3 tags and cover art; the original is never touched.

mood_samples

A built-in folder of free-to-use tracks, always ready, streamed and never copied to your device.

Private by design

Your library lives in IndexedDB. Nothing you import is ever uploaded to a server.

Interactive demo

Try the cover editor and the controls

Visual simulation: drag the canvas, adjust with the sliders and use the keyboard: Space N P.

mood_samples

Free music, always available

  1. Drop your audio files into public/mood_samples/, with any subfolders you like.
  2. Build: a script reads each track’s title, artist, duration and cover art and generates the catalog.
  3. Stream it: every visitor downloads only what they play, with nothing copied to their device.
  4. Publish for free on GitHub Pages with an automatic deploy on every push.
